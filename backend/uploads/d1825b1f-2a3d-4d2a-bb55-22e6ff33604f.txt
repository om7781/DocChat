--- ABOUT THE COMPANY ---

NovaTech Solutions is a software company founded in 2015, headquartered in Austin, Texas.
The company specializes in cloud infrastructure, AI-powered analytics, and cybersecurity tools.
As of 2026, NovaTech employs over 1,200 people across offices in Austin, London, and Singapore.
Annual revenue for 2025 was reported at $340 million, representing a 22% growth from the previous year.

--- LEAVE POLICY ---

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
Employees in their probation period (first 3 months) are not eligible for paid leave.
Sick leave is capped at 10 days per calendar year and requires a medical certificate for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks fully paid. Paternity leave is 4 weeks fully paid.
Unused annual leave of up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year.

--- SALARY & BENEFITS ---

Salaries are reviewed annually every April. Performance bonuses are paid in January.
The company offers health insurance covering employees and up to 3 dependents.
Employees are eligible for a 401(k) plan with a 5% company match after completing 6 months of service.
Remote work allowance of $100 per month is provided for employees working fully remotely.
All employees receive a $2,000 annual learning and development budget for courses and certifications.

--- CODE OF CONDUCT ---

Employees must maintain confidentiality of all client and company data.
Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the HR department within 7 days of arising.
Harassment of any kind — verbal, physical, or digital — is grounds for immediate termination.
All employees are required to complete the annual cybersecurity training by December 31st each year.

--- IT POLICY ---

Company laptops must not be used for personal activities such as gaming or streaming.
All software installations must be approved by the IT department.
Employees must use a VPN when accessing company systems outside the office.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must be at least 12 characters long.
